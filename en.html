About me

Hi, I’m Christian, the DJ behind LFGN.

I’m 35, I live in Hamburg and I spend a big part of my time with music. Behind the decks, at the piano, or hunting for the next track that lights up a dance floor.

Music has been part of my life for as long as I can remember. I started playing the piano as a kid and learned how melodies build tension and release it again. I bring that feel for harmony and timing into every set.

I bought my first mixer at 21. I’ve been DJing since 2012, first at friends’ parties, later at weddings, corporate events, openings and rooftop parties. What hasn’t changed is my aim: not just to play songs, but to shape an evening your guests will remember.

Before every gig we take time to talk through your evening: which songs are a must, which are a no-go, when is the first dance or the speech? On the night itself I respond to your guests instead of a fixed playlist.
